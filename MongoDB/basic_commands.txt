Create a database students collection: students with fields: name, age, course, city, and feesPaid.

1. Create:
Insert at least 5 student records
Use different courses and mix of feesPaid (true/false)
Ensure at least 2 students have the same name

use collegeDB
db.createCollection("students")
db.students.insertMany([{name:"studentA",age:20,course:"B.Tech",city:"Mathura",feesPaid:true},{name:"studentA",age:25,course:"BCA",city:"Agra",feesPaid:false},{name:"studentB",age:25,course:"B.Tech",city:"Agra",feesPaid:true},{name:"studentC",age:30,course:"BCA",city:"Aligarh",feesPaid:true},{name:"studentD",age:18,course:"BBA",city:"Mathura",feesPaid:false}])


2. Read:

Retrieve all students
db.students.find()

Find students with course = "BCA"
db.students.find({course:"BCA"})

Find students with age > 20
db.students.find({age:{$gt:20}})

Display only name and course fields
db.students.find({},{name:1,course:1})


3. Update:

Update city of one student
db.students.updateOne({name:"studentB"},{$set:{city:"Punjab"}})

Change course from BCA to MCA
db.students.updateOne({course:"BCA"},{$set:{course:"MCA"}})

Set feesPaid = true for students with age > 22
db.students.updateOne({age:{$gt:20}},{$set:{feesPaid:true}})


4. Delete:

Delete students with feesPaid = false
db.students.deleteMany({feesPaid:false})

Delete students with age < 20
db.students.deleteMany({age:{$lt:20}})
